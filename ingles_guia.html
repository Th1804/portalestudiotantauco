Unit 1: The Right to an Education

Aprende sobre el derecho a la educación, vocabulario escolar e igualdad de género en inglés.

🎓

What is education?

Education = el proceso de aprender y adquirir conocimientos, habilidades y valores. En inglés, hablamos de education como un derecho humano fundamental.

💡 Key concept: Article 26 of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights states: "Everyone has the right to education." (Artículo 26 de la Declaración Universal de Derechos Humanos dice: "Todos tienen derecho a la educación.")

Definición en inglés

Education is the process of learning and developing knowledge, skills, values, and attitudes. It can take place at school, at home, or in the community.

(La educación es el proceso de aprender y desarrollar conocimientos, habilidades, valores y actitudes. Puede ocurrir en la escuela, en casa o en la comunidad.)

Types of Education · Tipos de educación

EnglishEspañolExample sentence
Formal educationEducación formal"She received formal education at school." (Ella recibió educación formal en la escuela.)
Informal educationEducación informal"He learned cooking through informal education." (Aprendió a cocinar mediante educación informal.)
Higher educationEducación superior"She went to university for higher education." (Fue a la universidad para educación superior.)
Primary educationEducación primaria"All children should have access to primary education." (Todos los niños deben tener acceso a la educación primaria.)
🔊 Pronunciation Tips
education /ˌedʒuˈkeɪʃən/ = "ed-yu-KAY-shun"
knowledge /ˈnɒlɪdʒ/ = "NOL-ij" (la K es muda)
school /skuːl/ = "skul" (la CH no se pronuncia como en español)
⚠️ Common Mistakes
✗ "I studied in a school of Spain" → ✓ "I studied at a school in Spain"
✗ "Education is important for the society" → ✓ "Education is important for society" (sin "the")

Education as a Human Right

Education is recognized as a fundamental human right. This means every person, regardless of their gender, nationality, or economic situation, has the right to learn.

(La educación es reconocida como un derecho humano fundamental. Esto significa que toda persona, sin importar su género, nacionalidad o situación económica, tiene derecho a aprender.)

Useful Sentences · Oraciones útiles

🔵 "Education is a universal right." → La educación es un derecho universal.

🔵 "Every child deserves equal access to education." → Todo niño/a merece igual acceso a la educación.

🔵 "We must fight against discrimination in schools." → Debemos luchar contra la discriminación en las escuelas.

What stops people from going to school?

Around the world, many children and young people cannot access education. These are called barriers to education.

Types of Barriers · Tipos de barreras

💰
Economic barriers
Barreras económicas
When families cannot afford school fees, uniforms, or materials. Many children must work instead of studying.

Cuando las familias no pueden pagar matrículas, uniformes o materiales. Muchos niños deben trabajar en vez de estudiar.
👧
Gender barriers
Barreras de género
In some places, girls are not allowed to go to school due to cultural or social norms.

En algunos lugares, a las niñas no se les permite ir a la escuela por normas culturales o sociales.
📍
Geographic barriers
Barreras geográficas
Children in remote or rural areas may have no schools nearby. Distance makes education impossible without support.

Los niños en zonas remotas o rurales pueden no tener escuelas cercanas.
⚔️
Conflict & instability
Conflicto e inestabilidad
Wars and conflicts destroy schools and displace families, making education very difficult or impossible.

Las guerras y conflictos destruyen escuelas y desplazan familias.
💡 Mini summary: Barriers = things that prevent access to education. The main ones are: economic, gender-based, geographic, and conflict-related.

What is gender equality?

Gender equality means that people of all genders have the same rights, opportunities, and access — including in education. It is NOT about making men and women identical, but about giving everyone equal chances.

(Igualdad de género significa que personas de todos los géneros tienen los mismos derechos, oportunidades y acceso — incluyendo en educación.)

Gender Equality Vocabulary

EnglishEspañolExample

Why does gender equality in education matter?

  • Girls with education earn more money and have better health
  • Educated women contribute more to their communities
  • Education reduces poverty and inequality
  • Every child, regardless of gender, has a right to learn

Unit 2: Funding Climate Action

Aprende sobre el cambio climático, vocabulario ambiental y cómo se financia la acción climática.

🌍

What is climate change?

Climate change refers to long-term shifts in global temperatures and weather patterns, mainly caused by human activities like burning fossil fuels.

🌱 Remember: "Climate" (clima a largo plazo) ≠ "Weather" (tiempo atmosférico del día). Climate change = cambio en los patrones climáticos globales a largo plazo.

Definition · Definición

Climate change refers to significant, long-term changes in the global climate. It includes global warming, changes in precipitation, and more extreme weather events.

(Cambio climático: cambios significativos y a largo plazo en el clima global. Incluye el calentamiento global, cambios en las precipitaciones y eventos climáticos más extremos.)

Causes · Causas

Cause (English)Causa (Español)
Burning fossil fuels (coal, oil, gas)Quemar combustibles fósiles (carbón, petróleo, gas)
DeforestationDeforestación
Industrial pollutionContaminación industrial
Agriculture emissionsEmisiones agrícolas
Greenhouse gas emissionsEmisiones de gases de efecto invernadero

Effects · Efectos

  • Rising sea levels → Aumento del nivel del mar
  • More extreme weather → Clima más extremo (inundaciones, sequías)
  • Loss of biodiversity → Pérdida de biodiversidad
  • Melting glaciers and ice caps → Derretimiento de glaciares
  • Food and water shortages → Escasez de alimentos y agua
🔊 Pronunciation Tips
climate /ˈklaɪmɪt/ = "KLY-mit"
environment /ɪnˈvaɪərənmənt/ = "in-VY-er-on-ment"
sustainable /səˈsteɪnəbəl/ = "suh-STAY-nuh-bul"

What is climate action?

Climate action refers to efforts and initiatives taken to reduce greenhouse gas emissions and help communities adapt to climate change. It includes both individual actions and large-scale policies.

(Acción climática: esfuerzos e iniciativas para reducir emisiones de gases de efecto invernadero y ayudar a las comunidades a adaptarse al cambio climático.)

What does "funding" mean?

To fund = financiar (dar dinero para apoyar algo). Funding = financiamiento.

Climate funding means providing money and resources to support projects that fight climate change or help communities deal with its effects.

(Financiamiento climático significa proveer dinero y recursos para apoyar proyectos que combaten el cambio climático o ayudan a las comunidades a enfrentar sus efectos.)

Types of Climate Funding

  • Government investment → Inversión gubernamental en energías limpias
  • International funds → Fondos internacionales (ej. Green Climate Fund)
  • Private sector investment → Inversión del sector privado en tecnología verde
  • Community-based programs → Programas comunitarios de adaptación

Useful sentences

🌱 "Governments must invest in renewable energy." → Los gobiernos deben invertir en energía renovable.

🌱 "We need international cooperation to fund climate action." → Necesitamos cooperación internacional para financiar la acción climática.

🌱 "Developing countries need financial support." → Los países en desarrollo necesitan apoyo financiero.

🟢 Level 1 · Beginner — Unit 1
Every Child's Right
Education is a right for every child in the world. According to the United Nations, all children should have access to free and quality education. However, many children do not go to school. Some cannot go because their families are poor. Others live far from any school. In some countries, girls are not allowed to study. We must work together to make sure every child can learn.

Comprehension Questions — Passage 1

🟡 Level 2 · Intermediate — Unit 1
Breaking Barriers: Girls and Education
Around the world, approximately 130 million girls are out of school. This is a significant global challenge. Many girls face multiple barriers to education: poverty, child marriage, cultural norms, and lack of safe schools. Research shows that educating girls has enormous benefits. When girls are educated, they earn higher incomes, have healthier families, and contribute more to their communities. Organizations like UNESCO and UNICEF work to promote girls' education globally. Gender equality in education is not just a human right — it is also a smart investment for every nation's development.

Comprehension Questions — Passage 2

🟠 Level 3 · Intermediate — Unit 2
Funding the Green Revolution
Climate change is one of the most pressing challenges of the 21st century. Scientists agree that we must reduce greenhouse gas emissions dramatically to avoid the worst effects of global warming. However, taking action requires enormous financial resources. The Green Climate Fund, established in 2010, was created to support developing nations in their efforts to reduce emissions and adapt to climate impacts. Developed countries promised to provide 100 billion dollars per year by 2020. While progress has been made, many experts argue that current funding levels are still insufficient. Sustainable solutions require long-term investment in renewable energy, green infrastructure, and climate-resilient communities.
